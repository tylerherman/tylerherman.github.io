Experience

I’ve been a designer my whole career and have worked in a number of different industries. Everything from studios to internal design teams and from startups to fortune 100 companies. Doing design work for B2B and B2C customers as responsive web apps or for Android or iOS.

Simpson Strong-Tie
Senior UX/UI Designer

At Simpson Strong-Tie I work to launch Cloud Director, a workflow management platform for truss manufacturing plants. I design for a mix of office and production-floor users — schedulers, plant managers, builders — where mistakes carry real operational cost, not just UX friction. I've also built out AI-assisted prototyping workflows using v0, Figma Make, and Claude Code to move faster from concept to code-ready prototype, closing the gap between design and dev handoff.

AcuityMD
Design Lead

As the first design hire I worked on all areas of the application. When I started we began by expanding the application from a simple page app that was just a targeting platform to include opportunity management, doctor and hospital profiles, sales, scheduling, product setup and creation, contracting, and territory creation, planning, tracking and management. Essentially, moving the product towards the end-to-end management of the medical device sales process.

AgencyMVP
Design Lead

Allstate
Senior Product Designer

I started working on two claims applications: one to allow customers to submit damage photos and the second a video chat application for adjusters and customers to communicate and share damage and repair information. I also inherited a third product to reduce the cost of creating damage estimates for home and auto claims.

FareCompare
Product Designer
